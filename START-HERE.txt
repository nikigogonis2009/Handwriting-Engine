Handwriting Engine: start here
==============================

This is a web app that turns a person's own Apple Pencil handwriting into new handwriting for any text or math, and can
put it on a worksheet (PDF). It runs in the browser. There is also an MCP server so an AI assistant can use it.

If you are an AI assistant helping the person who gave you this zip:

1. Read CLAUDE.md and README.md in this folder before doing anything. They say how everything works and what not to do.
2. The handwriting has to be the person's own, so they have to write the samples (about 30 minutes, on an iPad with an
   Apple Pencil). You cannot do that part for them. To get the app in front of them:
     npm run serve        (needs Node 18+; then open http://<this computer's address>:8080 in Safari on the iPad,
                           on the same Wi-Fi)
   Walk them through the Teach tab, then the Write tab and the Sheet tab. docs/handwriting-engine-guide.pdf is a plain
   setup guide they can read too.
3. When they have taught it, they tap Export in the Teach tab. That saves my-handwriting.json, which is their handwriting.
   Keep it private: do not commit it, do not paste it anywhere.
4. To use it from you (an MCP server, plain Node, no packages):
     node handwriting-mcp.js --samples my-handwriting.json --format svg
   (handwriting-mcp-pdf.js also reads and fills PDFs.) Tools: write_text, write_batch, handwriting_status, and in the
   PDF build inspect_pdf and fill_pdf. See the MCP section of README.md.
5. Check every result before you use it. Digits are the weakest part. Write only what the person asks you to write, and
   do not fill in their name or anything they did not give you. Whether it is okay to hand in handwriting made this way
   is their decision, under their school's or workplace's rules.
