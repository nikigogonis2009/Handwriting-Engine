Handwriting Engine

Three steps
  1. Go through the rounds below, in order (about 30 minutes). Write each one on the solid line, then tap Next.
  2. When you are done, tap Download for my AI at the bottom left. It saves a zip with your handwriting and everything your AI needs.
  3. Give that zip to your AI and tell it: "Unzip this and read FOR-THE-AI.txt."
Write the highlighted word on the solid line. Bigger and a little slower works better: try to fill the shaded band.

What it learned

Each colour is one letter the engine cut out of your word. If a word looks wrong, tap it to write it again. To check the letters themselves, tap a letter in the grid above and cross out any example that does not look like it.

Write math like this: x^2, x_1, \frac{a}{b}, \sqrt{x}, \lim_{x \to 0}, \int_0^1 x\,dx, \sum_{i=1}^{n}, \sqrt[3]{x} (or sqrt(x), cubert(x)). Plain text inside math: \text{ so }. A space that stays: \ (or \quad); ordinary spaces are ignored, as in TeX. A new line starts a new row. Symbols you have not written yet are drawn for you. Plain paper looks best.

Tap the letter. It is replaced by another example of the same letter, and the example is left out from then on. Nothing else on the page changes.

Teach it your handwriting first, then come back here.
